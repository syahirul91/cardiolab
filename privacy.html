Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Summary

CardioLab does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app works offline.

Information we collect

None. CardioLab does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, health data or any other personal information, and it does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

A small amount of app data is saved only on your iPhone so the app works as expected:

  • Your quiz score, accuracy and streak

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

In-app purchases

CardioLab Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details or Apple ID information. Apple only tells the app whether CardioLab Pro has been purchased so it can be unlocked. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

CardioLab contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. It does not sell or share data with anyone.

External links

The app includes links to outside websites (for example the European Society of Cardiology guidelines) and a link that opens your email app to contact us. These websites and services have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children

CardioLab is an educational app intended for students and healthcare professionals. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
