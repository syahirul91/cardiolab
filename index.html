How do I restore CardioLab Pro?

Open the Pro tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase and works on all your iPhones that use that Apple ID.

What does CardioLab Pro include?

Heart sounds, systolic and diastolic murmurs and the CVS examination guide are free. Pro unlocks prosthetic valve murmurs, continuous and mixed murmurs, and quiz questions on every recording. There is no subscription.

I can't hear the heart sounds clearly

  • Use headphones — most heart sounds are below 200 Hz and are hard to hear on a phone speaker.
  • Turn the volume up and check that silent mode is not muting the app.
  • Slow playback to 0.5× or 0.25× and turn on Loop to focus on one part of the cycle.

Playback stopped by itself

CardioLab pauses when you receive a call, when another app plays audio, when headphones are disconnected or when you leave the app. Tap play to continue.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app works fully offline.

Where does the clinical content come from?

Clinical summaries and management notes are based on current European Society of Cardiology (ESC) guidelines. CardioLab is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ESC. Always refer to the full guidelines before making clinical decisions.
